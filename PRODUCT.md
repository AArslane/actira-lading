# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Owner or manager of an independent US med spa (1-3 locations), selling high-value aesthetic treatments, already running Meta Ads and generating inquiries. Reads this page on a phone, mid-day, after receiving an Instagram DM or cold email from Actira. Decides in under 30 seconds whether a 15-minute call is worth it.

## Product Purpose

Actira sells a lead-to-booking follow-up system: instant lead response, SMS/email follow-up sequences, missed-call recovery, consultation booking, reminders, no-show and dormant-lead reactivation. Target: 3 clients at ~$350/month.

Success for the visitor: they understand who this is for, what breaks between lead and booking, and book a 15-minute audit.

## Positioning

"You're already paying for the lead. We fix what happens after the lead arrives."

Not an AI agency, not an automation agency, not lead-gen, not social media. A follow-up layer, sold like infrastructure.

## Operating Context

- Acquisition: Instagram DMs, cold email from `getactira.com`, LinkedIn, Upwork. This page is the bio link and the email link; it is a credibility surface, not an acquisition channel.
- Solo operator, Toulouse, building the first client base. No case studies yet.
- Sister project `~/Dev/actira-site` is an unrelated French GEO offer; do not reuse its blue accent or French copy.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML/CSS, zero build, deployed on Vercel at `getactira.com`.
- All copy in served HTML; page must read fully with JavaScript disabled.
- No invented statistics, no fabricated ROI, no fake testimonials or client logos.
- Never claim a specific prospect has poor follow-up.
- Primary CTA: Calendly `calendly.com/actira`.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Actira. Domain `getactira.com` (landing + email send). `actira.fr` reserved for the French offer.
- Visual world pinned by the founder: black / charcoal / white, brushed-chrome accent only, lots of negative space, strong typography, Apple / Vercel / editorial minimalism. Geist.
- Banned: neon, gradients as decoration, purple AI branding, robots, brains, lightning bolts, funnels, glow, gold-on-black, stock photos, emoji.
- Voice: concise, specific, low-hype. No "10X", no buzzwords.

## Evidence on Hand

Zero clients on this offer. No metrics exist. Credibility comes from precise understanding of the operational problem, not from proof.

## Product Principles

1. Legible in 10 seconds on a phone: who it's for, what breaks, what to do.
2. Never promise what can't be shown.
3. Anything that delays outreach is noise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for all text, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, fully readable without JS.
